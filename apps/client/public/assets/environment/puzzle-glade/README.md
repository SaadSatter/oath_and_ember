# Puzzle Glade art

Sources and PixelLab job details: `Images/Sprites/Puzzle_Glade_Statue/provenance.json`.

- `statue-sit.png` — 10 frames of 160x128. Frames 0-8 are PixelLab's
  interpolation from the original statue with her chair beside her to her
  seated on it with the chair behind her (it slides in behind her as she
  sits); frame 9 is the seated pose itself, held once she sits. Frame
  canvases sit at world (430, 376) over the glade's statue.
- `dry-bush.png` — 64x48 dry bramble clump; six wall the chair into the
  north-east corner until Coco's fire burns them (fire reuses the Mirror
  Pond torch flame).
