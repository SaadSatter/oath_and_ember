## Main-branch corrections to older documentation

- Pinned main uses a rescue **flash** that kills the remaining ambush monsters together. Concurrent local water-dragon and bush-entry work was visible during research but is outside this main-branch plan; no water-dragon event is assigned.
- Sieg starts with Guard and Super Speed (`dash` internal ID), plus his normal sword attack. Heavy Break requires unlocking `heavy`. Coco starts with Ward, Slow Time, Flight and Black Hole plus level-one Fireball. No electric blast exists. Optional Blink and Fireball upgrades are listed separately. Telekinesis exists as an unlock definition but has no distinct server crate interaction in this commit; it gets no cast cue.
- Griffin is asleep in `NEST`, wakes through `WAKING`, and fires its current **mouth** beam. Older “eye beam” prose is superseded. Its continuous beam has a contact visual tail but **no final ground explosion**. Sentinel has a separate palm beam with emitted `golem_blast` contact effects and 64px blast radius.
- Current Griffin grooming consumes both tools when `CUT` begins (`toolsTaken`), then `DEPART` drops the wig; Coco collects it with E after `DONE`. Older prose saying tools remain and wig is automatically awarded at haircut end is stale. The defeat route also permits nest collection.
- Current maze key carrier is labeled Forest Wolf and drawn with the `forest_wolf_runner` visual override; its reused internal presentation type is `mossling`. It is a puzzle actor, not a sixth damaging guard. Current `ensureHedgeMaze` key interaction checks Sieg's role; generic inventory checks require range ≤64 and LOS. It does **not explicitly require both Slow Time and Super Speed or a 42px tag radius**, despite older documentation and the helpful on-screen chase hint. Sound must follow the accepted key claim, never infer a combo prerequisite.
- Current runner speed is 150 roaming, 430 fleeing, or 560 fleeing when a nearby Sieg is boosted; world Slow Time scales movement. No fixed 86px/s assumption is used for audio.
- Current `pushPondMirror` chooses rotation sign from bank-relative side. It uses continuous angles and refuses overlapping increments; there is no automatic orientation snap. Reflected shots may light any torch they actually hit; an unreflected direct shot cannot.
- Current final bridge switches use opposite **occupancy**; the predicate does not read E. Completion uses `ticksFor(0.767)` (46 ticks at 60Hz), superseding the README's 23-tick/hold-E wording. Coco's earlier golden switch does require `interactHeld`. The final shot validation uses the second bridge rectangle as cast-origin bounds; the README's tighter marked center pad is presentation guidance, not an exact 40×40 server predicate.
- Hub connections are the completed bridge → Goddess Shrine → maze/pond/Griffin, with the later Forest Ruins route opening after Chapter 0. Early checkpoint descriptions of temporary direct Forest Ruins entrances and “Griffin not included yet” are superseded by current code.
- Goddess revelation explains seven relics and that the Ember opens a door; it does not stage a physical portal. Her strength was already faded. The wig fixes her appearance, not all her powers. The killer, stranger identity, companion, mother's fate and full oath remain unresolved.

## Reading the mapping and mix values

Volumes are initial per-voice linear gain on a 0–1 SFX bus, assuming peaks are normalized to a common headroom target before mixing. They are review estimates, not measured loudness. Keep summed SFX peaks below clipping. World sounds attenuate by listener distance/map; local menus do not spatialize. “One-shot” means once per observed accepted transition, not every snapshot or render. Explicit silence rows intentionally assign **no asset** and zero volume. Where a row names a companion cue, its exact source and full download/license details appear in the asset catalog.

Edited loops require trimming/crossfades; none of these short source files is assumed to be a seamless loop as delivered. Stop/fade loops on phase exit, death, scene exit, disconnect, app suspension or room reset. Rate-limit repeated actions, cap overlapping enemy/fire voices and duck nonessential effects under dialogue. Reuse one feedback cue when reward, inventory and story flags describe the same event.

## 11. Observation gaps and requirements for later implementation

These are planning constraints, not implemented changes or a request to change gameplay.

1. **Snapshot baselines and duplicate prevention.** Initialize seen flags/IDs from the first room snapshot, as `StoryBanners` already does. Do not play every earned story/reward flag on late join, reconnect or branch revisit. Track combat by player+seq, enemy attacks by enemy+attackSeq, impacts by ID, beam contacts by contactId+impactClock, and rewards by newly claimed target. Keep local UI clicks separate from server success.
2. **Missing exact hit observations.** `defensiveHit.seq`, projectile impact IDs, story flags, beam contact fields and puzzle latches already provide useful edges. Sword victim contacts, some unguarded melee hits, lethal respawns and transient pond reflections can occur between snapshots. HP deltas identify hurt, not reliably which sword hit or every sub-tick contact. `movementEpoch` also means correction/recovery and must not be treated as a death flag. For reliable per-hit/per-reflection sound, later expose a minimal server-owned presentation event with stable ID/tick/source/target; protocol changes would require meaningful integration tests. Until approved, mark these cues as requiring that observation support rather than simulate hit truth on the client.
3. **Held scenes / two clocks.** Finale `hold` freezes enemy/Griffin/trap progress. Pause their charge/flight/machinery loops when their authoritative clock stops. Resume at the existing phase position. UI and story pacing remain normal. Follow actual remaining timers: Slow Time does not universally scale all cooldowns or effect lifetimes; the Black Hole vortex life in `tickBlackHoles` decreases by DT while travel/pull are scaled.
4. **Private story audio.** Gate story cues by current scene, cast/audience, and `storyView` visibility/watch behavior. Sieg must not hear Coco's private discovery/healing as though present, and Coco must not hear Sieg's remembered promise. Camera-watching and local hero map can differ; use the displayed scene and intended story audience together.
5. **Predicted versus accepted casts.** `castPrediction` may show an immediate local cast. A later audio implementation must either reconcile that preview with the accepted projectile or wait for acceptance; never play both. Black Hole also sets combat.kind=cast, so a generic cast hook must not play a Fireball cue for it.
6. **Contact identity.** A projectile disappearing is not necessarily an impact; it can expire or be cleared on travel/cutscene. Griffin beam has no terminal explosion. Its contact tail and Sentinel `golem_blast` must use different identities/timbres. Rows calling out contact-only cues require the existing impact record or later exact contact observation.
7. **Inventory acknowledgements.** A request/receipt or unchanged revision is not proof of a new pickup: repeated successful requests can return cached results. Compare claimed/unlocked/completed and actual counts/flags. Story `give`/`take` differs from generic chest receipt. Griffin delivery increments revision even if no new tool flag changes; sound only on the flag edge. Current nest UI sends no selected item and delivers all owned nest tools together.
8. **No mechanics derived from audio.** Sound never sets HP, cooldowns, positions, damage, story flags, puzzle latches or completion. Shared kinematics stay deterministic; no browser audio timer participates in simulation.
