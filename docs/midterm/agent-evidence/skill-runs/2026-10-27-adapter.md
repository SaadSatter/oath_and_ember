## Recommended tests

1. A gateway that always throws: the calls are counted in `modelCalls`, `policy_error` lines carry latency, and the episode ends with `error` or `model_call_limit` once the budget is used up.
2. A gateway that alternates failure and success: total attempts never exceed `maxModelCallsPerPlayer`.
3. A gateway that never resolves: the per-call timeout ends it, the call is counted, and `runEpisode` returns within `wallClockMs` plus a small margin.
4. A refusal with returned usage: the spend and tokens are counted.
5. Two concurrent gateways near the cap: the combined spend stays at or below `maxUsd`.
6. Runner-level `spend_limit` and `token_limit` terminal reasons.
7. A leak test: no observation contains `HP n/m` for the partner or enemies. Their health appears only at bar resolution.
8. The griffin is absent from `visible` after `groomingStage === "DONE"`.
9. The gate `use_item` enum matches what `InventoryPanel` lists.
