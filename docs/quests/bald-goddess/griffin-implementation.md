# Griffin Enclosure

Reach the final northern map with E at the Goddess Shrine's north trail. Its southern return leads back to the hub. The 40×36 top-down arena has water across the northern third, a small nest island against the northern wall, and four solid ruined pillars on land. Existing PixelLab water, grass, paving, walls, mirrors and trees are reused; overhead straw and pillar/rubble shapes are original geometry.

The supplied Griffin export is preserved untouched under Images/Sprites/Griffin and copied with metadata into Images/Sprites/monster_sprites/sources/griffin/pixellab-export. scripts/import-griffin.py packs native 164px takeoff, flying and running frames (116px idle views are centered with transparent padding) with stable anchors and nearest-neighbor filtering. No north-running animation was supplied; that direction retains its native standing view. All eight original rotations remain preserved.

The server owns the encounter state, HP, phase timers, locked attack directions, pillar collapses and reward. It patrols over water, warns with a fixed line before firing a blocked eye beam, and defends the nest when flying Coco approaches. Black Hole interrupts flight and physically pulls it ashore, with solid obstacles respected. Landed Griffin headbutts after Sieg remains stationary; the warning locks its direction, allowing a dodge or a pillar lure. A swept pillar contact collapses only that pillar and stuns the guardian for six world seconds. Slow Time scales flight, attack warnings, beam/headbutt durations, pull and stun timing to 20 percent. Existing Super Speed and teammate pushing retain their shared rules. Sword attacks require a grounded Griffin and a clear frontal arc; fireballs and Black Hole can damage it. Defeat stops attacks and permits collecting shed plumage at the nest.

The peaceful solution uses the earned shared Vanity Mirror and Enchanted Shears. Flying Coco reaches the nest, selects the mirror in inventory and uses it on the nest, creating a 20-world-second distraction. Using the shears during that distraction awards one shared Griffin Wig without consuming either tool or requiring a kill. Return south and offer the wig at the goddess. Server inventory checks role, scene, range, sight, item ownership, order and request replay; completed rewards cannot be farmed after offering or revisiting. Combat/pillar/quest progress persists through hub travel.

Validation includes an intent-only spawn-to-nest flight and return with tools from prior branch reward fixtures; water/solid flight collisions; AI pull over live ticks; beam warning, dodge, occlusion and single hit; stationary bait and swept pillar stun; Slow Time; nest defense; wrong/ranged/missing/replayed offerings; defeated reward; full shrine handoff; and two real Socket.IO clients receiving matching encounter/inventory state. scripts/qa-griffin.ts additionally checks two Chrome clients, actual pillar collision and inventory UI delivery.
